BUILD 088 - TOOLKIT 8080 WIFI SERVICE CONTROLS
Date: 2026-08-31

Purpose:
Apply the service-safe controls to the actual technician WiFi Manager on Toolkit port 8080.

Controls:
- EGYM 2.4 GHz: EGYM AAN / EGYM UIT.
- Matrix service 5 GHz: SERVICE WIFI AAN; no normal disable button.
- Matrix service SSID: SSID VERBERGEN / SSID ZICHTBAAR.
- Restore: egym ON + <hostname>_5G ON and hidden.
