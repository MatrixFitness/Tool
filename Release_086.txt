BUILD 086 - WIFI SERVICE SAFE
Date: 2026-08-30

Purpose:
Make field WiFi testing safe: EGYM 2.4 GHz can be switched independently while Matrix service 5 GHz remains available.

Behavior:
- EGYM 2.4 GHz: independent ON/OFF.
- Matrix service 5 GHz: independent ON/OFF with explicit disconnect warning.
- Matrix service 5 GHz: hidden/visible control; hidden by default.
- Restore defaults: 2.4 GHz egym ON; 5 GHz <hostname>_5G ON and hidden.
