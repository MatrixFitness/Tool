BUILD 116 - CONNECTION EMAIL FORM REFRESH PAUSE
Date: 2026-09-25

Purpose:
Keep entered email information and report selections intact while a connection test is running.

Changes:
- Keeps the ten-second progress refresh active while the email form is untouched.
- Pauses automatic refresh immediately after typing, changing language or selecting a report.
- Preserves recipient, email address, customer, sender, note, language and report selections.
- Shows a clear notice when automatic refresh has been paused.
