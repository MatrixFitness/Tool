BUILD 124 - CUSTOMER ETHERNET WAN-ONLY TESTING
Date: 2026-10-12

Purpose:
Test and explain the club customer Ethernet WAN independently from the Matrix 5G connection.

Changes:
- Binds the customer connectivity measurement to Ethernet WAN and excludes Matrix 5G from the WAN score.
- Measures WAN link, WAN address, customer gateway, public internet targets and DNS every minute.
- Explains the most likely problem area in plain language for the club and its IT provider.
- Provides concrete checks for cabling, DHCP/VLAN, gateway, provider/firewall and DNS problems.
- Keeps JDKMatrix reports in English and other router reports in Dutch.
