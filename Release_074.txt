BUILD 074 - PERSISTENT NETWORK STANDARD
Date: 2026-08-24

Purpose:
Make the proven Matrix network-standard policy reliable on JNL, JBE and JDE without requiring a manual command after an update.

Changes:
- Installs and enables /etc/init.d/matrix_network_standard as a one-shot boot service.
- Runs matrix-network-standard immediately after the complete Toolkit payload and CLI files are installed.
- Keeps the later transactional verification as a second safety check.
- 2.4 GHz AP is forced enabled with SSID egym.
- 5 GHz AP is forced enabled as JNLMatrix_5G, JBEMatrix_5G or JDEMatrix_5G based on hostname.
- Mobile SIM1 remains primary with metric 1.
- Ethernet WAN remains enabled as fallback with metric 10.
- Old WiFi STA/client profiles remain disabled.
- The policy is re-applied after router reboot, including after firmware upgrades.
