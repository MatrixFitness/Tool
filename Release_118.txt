BUILD 118 - VALIDATED EML PDF ATTACHMENTS
Date: 2026-09-25

Purpose:
Prevent empty or incomplete PDF attachments in downloaded email drafts.

Changes:
- Validates readability, size and the PDF header of every selected report.
- Builds the complete MIME email in a temporary file before offering the download.
- Confirms that every selected attachment is present and that the EML is larger than the source PDFs.
- Blocks incomplete downloads and shows a clear error instead.
- Displays the source PDF size beside every report.
